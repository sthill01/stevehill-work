Day 120 — what we agree this looks like
01
Proper tracking setup
Every brand, every channel, every conversion event tagged with the right convention. UTM, GTM, ServiceTitan Campaign + Job Tag fields, call-source mapping.
02
Systems communicating
Ad platforms, GA4 / GTM, CallRail, ServiceTitan, revenue, reviews — all reconcile through agreed data contracts.
03
Reporting + dashboards
One portfolio scorecard plus role-specific Power BI views. Daily refresh on the pilot. Every dashboard names a decision owner.
04
Leakage identification
When a metric drops, the system tells us WHERE: media, intake, booking, dispatch, field ops, billing, or follow-up.
Strategy · Pilot, then Replicate Ten brands is too many to attack in parallel. One brand fully through the model first (selected from a Phase 1 commonality matrix), then cascade — risk shrinks, trust compounds, governance gets negotiated with one GM not ten.

Deliverables by 30-day gate
Phase 1 — Inventory + Pilot Selection
DAY 30
  • Brand Stack Inventory & Standardization Matrix — every brand's tooling in one comparable view
  • Tracking Audit — prioritized gap list (impact × ease of fix)
  • Growth Baseline Scorecard (all 10 brands) — spend, calls/day, services booked/day, CAC, ROAS, with traffic-light confidence ratings
  • Commonality Matrix — brand scoring on trade, spend, stack, GM receptivity, representativeness
  • Pilot Brand Selection Memo — recommendation + sign-off (CMO, pilot GM, IT)
Phase 2 — Stabilize on Pilot
DAY 60
  • Pilot Governance Proposal — UTM, GTM, ServiceTitan Campaign/Job Tag convention, designed to extend to portfolio, signed off by pilot GM + IT
  • Pilot Scorecard v2 — cleaned, automated, daily refresh
  • Pilot Weekly Dashboard — Power BI, production-grade, templated for replication
  • Replication Playbook v0 — step-by-step guide for applying the model to brand 2
Phase 3 — Operationalize + Replicate
DAY 90
  • Weekly Growth Review (Pilot) — standing Friday cadence, "scale / fix / test" framework
  • Closed-Loop Attribution v0 (Pilot) — ad spend → completed service in ServiceTitan
  • Leakage Layer Analysis (Pilot) — where qualified demand exits the funnel
  • Brand 2 Phase 2 outputs — governance + scorecard + dashboard live
  • Replication Playbook v1 — refined from brand 2 application
Phase 4 — Scale + Pilot Outcomes
DAY 120
  • Brand Efficiency Benchmark v1 (Pilot) — CAC, ROAS, revenue-per-lead, with trade and channel cuts
  • Budget Move Board (Pilot) — first defensible shifts, ownership, review dates
  • Brands 2–3 Phase 2 outputs — governance + scorecard + dashboard live
  • Replication Playbook v2 — refined from three applications, ready for portfolio rollout
  • Stack Standardization Roadmap — cascade order + next 90–180 days of priorities
